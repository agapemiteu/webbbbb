PRIVACY

Webb privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Webb is a Chrome extension that follows spoken instructions and helps carry them out on the web page you select. This policy explains what Webb handles when you use TALK or FOLLOW.

Data Webb handles

  • Audio: Webb captures microphone audio in TALK or audio from the tutorial tab you choose in FOLLOW, only after you start that mode. Audio is streamed to AssemblyAI for speech transcription.
  • Speech transcripts: Transcripts from you or the followed tutorial are sent to Webb's Cloudflare Worker. The Worker may send the transcript to Groq to classify the instruction and propose a browser action.
  • Page context: When planning an action, Webb sends the target page URL, title, and visible interactive control roles and labels to the Worker and Groq. Existing form field values and password values are excluded from this page context. Webb does not fill password fields.
  • Session state: Cloudflare stores the transcript, procedure progress, page context, and action results for the active session. Session data expires after 24 hours without activity. You can withdraw consent in Webb's settings to stop capture and request deletion of the active session.
  • Local settings: Chrome local storage holds your Worker URL, consent choice, and an identifier for your active session.

Why data is used

Webb uses this data to transcribe the speech you chose, match instructions to controls on your selected page, keep procedure progress, and verify browser actions. Webb does not sell data, use it for advertising, or build profiles for unrelated purposes.

Service providers

AssemblyAI processes audio to produce transcripts. Groq processes transcript and page context to produce a structured action proposal. Cloudflare hosts Webb's API and temporary session storage. These providers process data to provide Webb's requested features under their own service terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

Before Webb starts capture or page inspection, it shows a data disclosure and asks you to agree. You can stop TALK or FOLLOW at any time. In settings, choose Withdraw consent and stop Webb to stop capture and request deletion of the current cloud session. Data already sent may remain with the service providers under their applicable retention policies. Webb's own session data is scheduled to expire within 24 hours after its last activity.

Security

Provider API keys stay on the Worker. Audio and requests to the hosted Worker use encrypted connections. The extension acts only on the page you select, and consequential actions require direct user confirmation.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Webb publisher through the support contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing.
